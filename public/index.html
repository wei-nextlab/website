NEXT Lab

NEXT Lab

Next Generation Computing and Sensing Lab

We build efficient systems for Generative AI models (LLM, VLM, ASR, Omini, VLA, Diffusion, etc.) inference and training, advance domain-specific architecture (DSA) design for scientific computing, and develop mixed-signal circuit solutions (analog, digital, and optics) for sensing systems.

About the PI

Tao Wei leads NEXT Lab. He began as an Assistant Professor at the University of Rhode Island in 2012, was promoted to Associate Professor in 2017, and to Full Professor in 2022. As of 2023, he serves as a Professor and Director of NEXT Lab, coordinates the Clemson Cadence Project, and holds an adjunct position at the University of Rhode Island.

His research is funded by NSF, ONR, NASA, and private industry partners, and he received the ONR YIP award in 2017. A key highlight is FastFlowLM Inc., an NSF-sponsored spinoff from NEXT Lab co-founded by the PI, which later joined AMD and became ROCm/FastFlowLM.

Life in the Lab

Research Interests

  • Efficient Systems for LLMs: End-to-end system design for efficient Generative AI models (LLM, VLM, ASR, Omini, VLA, Diffusion, etc.) inference and training, with emphasis on latency, throughput, memory efficiency, and deployment on resource-constrained platforms.
  • Domain-Specific Architecture (DSA) for Scientific Computing: Architecture and accelerator co-design for scientific workloads, including model-driven and optimization-centric computation.
  • Mixed-Signal Circuit Design for Sensing Systems: Analog, digital, and optical circuit/system design for sensor and sensing applications.
