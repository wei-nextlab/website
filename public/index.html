NEXT Lab

Next Generation Computing and Sensing Lab

We build efficient systems for Generative AI models inference and training (LLM, VLM, ASR, Omni, ImageGen, etc.), advance domain-specific architecture (DSA) design for scientific computing, and develop mixed-signal circuit solutions (analog, digital, and optics) for sensing systems.

Explore Research Latest Updates

Life in the Lab

Research Interests

  • Efficient Systems for LLMs: End-to-end system design for efficient Generative AI models inference and training (LLM, VLM, ASR, Omni, ImageGen, etc.), with emphasis on latency, throughput, memory efficiency, and deployment on resource-constrained platforms.
  • Domain-Specific Architecture (DSA) for Scientific Computing: Architecture and accelerator co-design for scientific workloads, including model-driven and optimization-centric computation.
  • Mixed-Signal Circuit Design for Sensing Systems: Analog, digital, and optical circuit/system design for sensor and sensing applications.
