NEXT Lab: Efficient AI Systems, Architecture, and Impact

We design AI systems and accelerator architectures that make generative AI practical for client and edge platforms. Our work bridges research artifacts, hardware-software co-design, and real production translation.

Research Program About Tao Wei

What We Focus On

AI Systems for Efficient Inference

Runtime systems and kernel design for long-context, low-latency, and power-efficient generative AI workloads.

Architecture and Co-Design

Accelerator-aware software and NPU/FPGA architecture exploration to improve memory efficiency, throughput, and deployment practicality.

Open Research to Production

Projects like FastFlowLM demonstrate a complete path from lab research to industrial software ecosystems and semiconductor impact.

Featured Highlight: FastFlowLM

FastFlowLM provides the systems artifact for this research direction: an NPU-focused inference stack with clear evidence of industrial adoption and technology transfer.

News & Updates

Recent milestones from NEXT Lab (aligned with the Clemson NEXT Lab updates page).

  • 12/07/2025 NEXT Lab received DOE funding with Clemson, Brown, and ANL collaborators for edge ML accelerators.
  • 11/16/2025 Team members presented joint work with DOE ANL collaborators at SC25.
  • 10/01/2025 FastFlowLM was integrated into AMD's Lemonade Server platform.
  • 08/19/2025 NSF funding awarded for scalable next-generation silicon photonic design framework.
  • 06/25/2025 FastFlowLM project launched publicly as an AMD NPU-optimized inference engine.
  • 04/20/2025 NEXT Lab received NVIDIA H200 cluster resources through CU-MRI.
  • 09/01/2024 NSF planning funds awarded for efficient long-sequence ML models via algorithm-HW co-design.
  • 08/01/2024 ONR project started on ML-aided EDA for power electronics design.
  • 06/09/2023 NEXT Lab transitioned to Clemson University and launched its Clemson-era website.
