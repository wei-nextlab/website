Systems Artifact

The lab builds accelerator-aware software and evaluates NPU-class architectures to improve efficiency across memory, latency, and throughput.

Production Translation

Collaboration and integration with AMD/ROCm provide concrete evidence that research outcomes reached production semiconductor technology.

Why This Matters

  • Develops students and collaborators across ECE systems, architecture, and AI software.
