AI Systems and Architecture for Efficient Generative AI

The research agenda focuses on making Generative AI models (LLM, VLM, ASR, Omni, ImageGen, etc.) efficient across client, edge, and accelerator-rich environments. It combines software runtime design with architecture-level optimization and measurable real-world translation.

Program Components

1) Systems Artifact: FastFlowLM

An NPU-first runtime and kernel stack showing practical pathways to low-latency, energy-aware generative inference.

2) Hardware-Software Co-Design

NPU-native kernels, scheduling, and memory-aware execution strategies that align model behavior with accelerator constraints.

3) Technology Transfer

Integration into AMD platforms and ROCm ecosystem demonstrates movement from university research to production semiconductor software.

Representative Themes

  • Memory-centric execution for long-context and generative workloads.
  • Quantization- and bandwidth-aware kernel generation for NPUs.
  • Runtime systems for predictable latency, throughput, and power envelopes.
  • Cross-layer evaluation spanning model behavior, compiler/runtime, and accelerator architecture.

Evidence of Impact

FastFlowLM adoption in AMD's Lemonade Server and migration into ROCm indicate strong practical relevance and reproducible impact beyond publication.
