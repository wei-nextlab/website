Tao Wei

Professor of ECE at Clemson University and director of the NEXT Lab, with a focus on AI systems and architecture for efficient generative AI.

Positioning

The core differentiator is a tightly connected systems-to-architecture pipeline: a validated software artifact (FastFlowLM), NPU-native co-design, and demonstrated production translation through AMD/ROCm.

Selected Focus Areas

  • Domain-specialized accelerator architecture and evaluation.
  • Runtime systems and kernels for efficient generative inference.
  • Edge/client AI deployment under strict memory and power constraints.
  • Bridging open-source research tools and industrial semiconductor software stacks.

Research Impact Signals

  • FastFlowLM integration trajectory into AMD's software stack.
  • Cross-domain collaborations supported by NSF and DOE projects.
  • Mentorship and publication outcomes across systems and architecture venues.
