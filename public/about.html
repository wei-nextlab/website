Tao Wei

Professor of ECE at Clemson University and director of the NEXT Lab, with a primary research focus on Efficient Systems for LLMs (Inference and Training).

Positioning

The core differentiator is a tightly connected systems-to-architecture pipeline centered on LLM efficiency: runtime and kernel co-design, architecture-aware optimization, and translation to practical deployment settings.

Selected Focus Areas

  • Efficient Systems for LLMs (Inference and Training) with emphasis on latency, throughput, and memory efficiency.
  • Domain-Specific Architecture (DSA) design and evaluation for scientific computing.
  • Mixed-signal circuit design (analog, digital, optics) for sensing systems.
  • Edge/client AI deployment under strict memory and power constraints.

Research Impact Signals

  • Cross-domain collaborations supported by NSF, DOE, ONR, and industry projects.
  • Measurable software and architecture outcomes for efficient AI deployment.
  • Mentorship and publication outcomes across systems and architecture venues.
