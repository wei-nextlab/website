Tao Wei

AI Systems and Architecture for Efficient Generative AI

Tao Wei is Professor of ECE and directs the NEXT Lab, focusing on domain-specialized accelerators, reconfigurable computing, and applied machine learning. Tao leads research that couples kernel- and runtime-level systems artifacts (e.g. FastFlowLM) with hardware-aware architectures to make generative AI practical and efficient on client and edge platforms.

Positioning

Tao's work is positioned at the intersection of systems, architecture, and production semiconductor impact. Key elements:

  • Systems artifact: FastFlowLM — an open-source NPU-first runtime demonstrating how careful kernel and runtime design enables low-power, high-throughput generative inference.
  • Hardware-software co-design: NPU-native inference and accelerator-aware kernels developed with IRON and Lemonade to extract efficiency from modern NPUs.
  • Technology transfer: The FastFlowLM team's integration into AMD and upstreaming into ROCm provide concrete evidence of research-to-production translation — an important differentiator for senior ECE systems roles.

Selected impact

  • FastFlowLM integrated into AMD's Lemonade Server and the team joined AMD to accelerate on-device inference.
  • Multiple NSF and DOE collaborations advancing edge ML accelerators and photonic design.
  • Student mentorship and numerous publications in systems and accelerator conferences.
