FastFlowLM — Story & Impact

This page summarizes the FastFlowLM project and its impact. Full source: ROCm/FastFlowLM/flm_story.md.

Summary

FastFlowLM began as an NSF-funded university project and evolved into an open-source NPU-first runtime that was integrated with AMD's inference stack and later joined AMD. The work demonstrates systems-first approaches to efficient generative AI on NPUs, with clear production translation.
