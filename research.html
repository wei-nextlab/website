AI Systems and Architecture for Efficient Generative AI

This research program emphasizes end-to-end systems and architecture for running large generative models efficiently at scale and on client platforms. The work combines three strengths that position it for senior ECE systems roles:

  1. Systems artifact: FastFlowLM — an open-source NPU-first runtime that demonstrates software and kernel strategies for power- and area-efficient inference.
  2. Hardware–software co-design: NPU-native inference and accelerator-aware kernels that extract throughput and energy efficiency from domain-specific accelerators.
  3. Technology transfer and production impact: The FastFlowLM team's integration with AMD and subsequent incorporation into production platforms provides concrete evidence of translation from research to semiconductor products.

Research themes

  • Domain-specific kernel design for NPUs and reconfigurable fabrics
  • Memory-centric, long-context model execution for generative AI
  • End-to-end runtime systems that prioritize efficient quantized inference and low-latency generation
  • Architecture design and evaluation of NPU-class accelerators for client and edge platforms
  • Bridging open-source tools and production stacks to accelerate adoption

Evidence of impact

FastFlowLM's adoption in AMD's Lemonade Server, the team's hire into AMD, and migration of the codebase into the ROCm organization are strong signals that the research translated into production-grade software and semiconductor enablement.
